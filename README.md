### Hi, I'm Kenny 👋

I'm a gamer who builds free tools I'd want to use myself, and I'm learning decompilation and reverse engineering along the way. Everything here is original code and documentation, with no ROMs, game files or copyrighted code.

**World of Warcraft: Forever addons** (free on [Wago](https://addons.wago.io) and [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/members/masterred92/projects))
- [Dwarf Explorer's Bible](https://github.com/masterred92/DwarfExplorersBible): pick a material and see everything it crafts into, with auction prices, profit and crafting chains, like Terraria's Guide
- [NeutralFlip](https://github.com/masterred92/NeutralFlip): finds buy-low, sell-high gaps between your faction auction house and the neutral one ([Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/neutralflip))
- [PriceLog](https://github.com/masterred92/PriceLog): keeps a history of auction prices as you search ([Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/pricelog))
- [MatTooltip](https://github.com/masterred92/MatTooltip): tells you on the tooltip whether to sell, hold or vendor your materials ([Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/mattooltip))
- [GoldPerSpot](https://github.com/masterred92/GoldPerSpot): tracks how much gold per hour each farming spot really makes ([Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/goldperspot))

*Plugins for the big raid and questing addons:*
- [BRBoss](https://github.com/masterred92/BRBoss): `/brb 5` starts a DBM or BigWigs break bar and ends it with a vanilla boss line
- [WipeAndWin](https://github.com/masterred92/WipeAndWin): vanilla boss lines as DBM and BigWigs victory and wipe sounds
- [FloorIsLava](https://github.com/masterred92/FloorIsLava): a Details! board of who took the most avoidable damage
- [QuestieJobsDone](https://github.com/masterred92/QuestieJobsDone): the peon's "Job's done!" when a Questie quest is finished
- [ParchmentBars](https://github.com/masterred92/ParchmentBars): an old-parchment bar style for BigWigs
- [GruntCount](https://github.com/masterred92/GruntCount): BigWigs countdowns voiced by critters and peons

**EA SPORTS FC**
