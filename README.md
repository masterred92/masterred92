### Hi, I'm Kenny 👋

I'm learning decompilation and reverse engineering from the ground up: how compiled games and programs work, and how to turn them back into readable source. I document everything as I go.

I'm focusing on GBA games, because the variety of design across so many developers gives me a smorgasbord to learn from.

**What I'm working on**
- [decomp-library](https://github.com/masterred92/decomp-library): my learning library on decompilation tools, techniques, game projects and study paths
- [gba-decomp-toolkit](https://github.com/masterred92/gba-decomp-toolkit): a bring-your-own-ROM toolkit for setting up matching GBA decomp projects
- [decomp-toolkit-scripts](https://github.com/masterred92/decomp-toolkit-scripts): setup and helper scripts for a decomp workstation
- [decomp-practice](https://github.com/masterred92/decomp-practice): original C exercises for practising matching on x86, MIPS and PowerPC

**World of Warcraft: Forever addons** (free on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/members/masterred92/projects) and Wago)
- [NeutralFlip](https://github.com/masterred92/NeutralFlip): finds buy-low, sell-high gaps between your faction auction house and the neutral one ([Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/neutralflip))
- [PriceLog](https://github.com/masterred92/PriceLog): keeps a history of auction prices as you search ([Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/pricelog))
- [MatTooltip](https://github.com/masterred92/MatTooltip): tells you on the tooltip whether to sell, hold or vendor your materials ([Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/mattooltip))
- [GoldPerSpot](https://github.com/masterred92/GoldPerSpot): tracks how much gold per hour each farming spot really makes ([Wago](https://addons.wago.io/addons/goldperspot))
- [Recipe Master](https://github.com/masterred92/RecipeMaster): pick a material and see everything it crafts into, with auction prices, profit and crafting chains, like Terraria's Guide (coming to Wago at launch, Nov 4)

*Plugins for the big raid and questing addons (coming to Wago at launch, Nov 4):*
- [BRBoss](https://github.com/masterred92/BRBoss): `/brb 5` starts a DBM or BigWigs break bar and ends it with a vanilla boss line
- [WipeAndWin](https://github.com/masterred92/WipeAndWin): vanilla boss lines as DBM and BigWigs victory and wipe sounds
- [FloorIsLava](https://github.com/masterred92/FloorIsLava): a Details! board of who took the most avoidable damage

- [GruntCount](https://github.com/masterred92/GruntCount): BigWigs countdowns voiced by critters and peons
